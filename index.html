Profile

Where health policy
meets technology.

Sulayman Abu-Bakr is a health informatics and health systems strengthening leader with more than ten years of progressive experience in Nigeria's health sector. His work spans national health policy, primary health care (PHC) transformation, ICT integration and health insurance reform.

As Executive Secretary and CEO of the Niger State Contributory Health Agency (NiCare) since 4 January 2024, he has grown health insurance enrolment by 83.3%, and the Agency has been recognised nationally as one of the top five performing state social health insurance agencies.

Before NiCare, he spent nearly a decade at the National Primary Health Care Development Agency (NPHCDA). There he led large-scale digital health initiatives, including the Electronic Management of Immunization Data (EMID) system and the deployment of the DHIS2 platform. His training in business information systems and public health lets him work across policy, technology and data, with a consistent focus on measurable improvements in healthcare access and quality.

03

Experience

A career built on
public service.

Jan 2024 — Present

Niger State Contributory Health Agency (NiCare)

Visit NiCare →

Executive Secretary / Chief Executive Officer

Sulayman leads the Agency's day-to-day administration and staff, with universal health coverage for Niger State as the goal. Under his leadership, enrolment grew by 83.3% and coverage for vulnerable persons by approximately 685% between 2023 and 2025. He has introduced schemes for students, inmates and retirees, and a Gavi/UNICEF/CHAI-supported package for women and children. He also restructured the Agency, digitised its claims and fund management, and oversees its budget so that programmes stay financially sustainable and in line with national health policy.

Jan 2021 — Jan 2024

National Primary Health Care Development Agency (NPHCDA)

Senior Program Analyst

As Senior Program Analyst, he led the data analysis and systems design behind the Agency's new and existing data processing systems. He strengthened the primary health care system by deploying ICT solutions for data management, led the Electronic Management of Immunization Data (EMID) activities, and provided training and technical support to states and Local Government Areas. In 2024 he was promoted to Assistant Chief Program Analyst in recognition of his sustained performance.

Jan 2018 — Jan 2021

National Primary Health Care Development Agency (NPHCDA)

Program Analyst I

In this period he developed tools for the Basic Health Care Provision Fund (BHCPF) NPHCDA Gateway. During the pandemic he supported the NPHCDA COVID-19 Crisis Management Centre, building health information management tools for community support centres. He was also selected as a Fellow of the US CDC's Growing Expertise in E-Health Knowledge and Skills (GEEKS) programme, where he managed the national DHIS2 backend server and led DHIS2 system administration training.

Dec 2014 — Jan 2018

National Primary Health Care Development Agency (NPHCDA)

Program Analyst II

He began his career at NPHCDA developing the scorecard for implementing the Primary Health Care Under One Roof (PHCUOR) initiative and managing IT solutions and reporting for the Executive Director's office. He also built the Agency's social media presence into an advocacy tool, coordinating initiatives with development partners including UNICEF, WHO and EU-SIGN.

05

Leadership & Service

Shaping health policy
at the table.

He serves on technical working groups and committees that guide health financing, disease control and emergency response in Niger State and nationally.

Sulayman chairs the Niger State Health Financing Technical Working Group and sits on the Basic Health Care Provision Fund State Oversight Committee and the UNDP North-West & Niger State Steering Committee.

In Niger State, he is a member of the Viral Hepatitis Technical Working Group, the State Emergency Treatment Committees, and the State Emergency Medical Service and Ambulance System. He also served on the coordination committee for the 2025 Integrated Insecticide-Treated Nets Replacement and Seasonal Malaria Chemoprevention Campaign.

At national level, he was part of the strategy group for Nigeria's COVID-19 vaccination rollout (COVAX). He has also served on the Monitoring & Evaluation Technical Working Group, the PHC Facilities Assessment Team, and the teams responsible for implementing EMID and the routine immunization transition.

In May 2026 he co-organised, with the Niger State Ministry of Health and World Bank support, a two-day policy dialogue in Bida on advancing universal health coverage through strategic leadership by Local Government Areas.

06

Education & Credentials

A foundation in
health and technology.

Education

Sulayman holds a Master of Public Health from Nile University of Nigeria, awarded with Distinction in 2023, where he also served as course representative for his cohort. His academic foundation is in technology: a BSc in Business Information Systems from the University of East London (2010–2013), followed by Coventry University's Master's Qualifying Programme in 2013.

Continuing Development

He continues to build on this foundation. In 2026 he took part in the African Centre for Tax and Governance executive programme Leading Public Sector Transformation in the Age of Artificial Intelligence in Kigali, Rwanda. In 2025 he completed Futures for Public Policy at the Lee Kuan Yew School of Public Policy, National University of Singapore, and Health Financing Policy for Universal Health Coverage with the World Health Organization. He trained in artificial intelligence in health insurance in 2024 and completed Tiers 1 and 2 of the US CDC's GEEKS programme in 2020. He participated in the First International Conference on Public Health in Africa, convened by the African Union and Africa CDC in 2021, and studied social media in healthcare at Taipei Medical University and the University of Southampton in 2017. In 2026 he became a member of the European Centre for Research Training and Development UK.

Recognition

Under his leadership, NiCare has been recognised nationally as one of the top five performing state social health insurance agencies. In September 2026 he received a Certificate of Recognition from the Female Director Generals of Niger State at their Awards & Appreciation Dinner in Minna, and an Award of Excellence from the Niger State National Health Fellows for strengthening healthcare delivery. In 2025 Crusader Radio, Minna, presented him with its Award for Excellence in Service to Humanity at its 7th Anniversary Awards. Earlier in his career, NPHCDA awarded him a Certificate of Recognition in 2020, and he was selected as a US CDC GEEKS Fellow for 2019–2020.
