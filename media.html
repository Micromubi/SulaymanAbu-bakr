Sep 2026

Minna

Recognition

Certificate of Recognition from the Female Director Generals of Niger State

Honoured at the Female Director Generals' Awards & Appreciation Dinner on 16 September at the Niger State Teachers Professional Development Institute, Minna, themed around recognising service, empowering people and building a stronger Niger State.

This Certificate of Recognition is not mine alone. It belongs to the entire NiCare team, our Health Financing Technical Working Group, and every partner working with us.

Aug 2026

Abuja

Conference

6th SPHPN & 37th PAPHNON Joint Conference

Represented NiCare and Niger State at the joint conference of the Society for Public Health Professionals of Nigeria and the Professional Association of Public Health Nurses of Nigeria, themed Public Health in a Changing World: Innovation, Equity and Global Health Security.

Aug 2026

Kigali, Rwanda

Executive Education

Leading Public Sector Transformation in the Age of Artificial Intelligence

Took part in the African Centre for Tax and Governance (ACTG) executive training, including a close look at IREMBO, Rwanda's one-stop platform for government services, and drew lessons for health insurance administration in Niger State.

Technology adoption is only half the story. The real work is building the institutional readiness (data, people, systems, leadership) to use it responsibly.

Aug 2026

Minna

Coverage

First Lady and 25 LGA Vice Chairpersons unveiled as Health Insurance Ambassadors

NiCare unveiled Her Excellency, Hajiya Fatima Mohammed Bago, First Lady of Niger State, and the Vice Chairpersons of all 25 Local Government Areas as Health Insurance Ambassadors. The event also flagged off the drive to enrol 65,000 more vulnerable residents under the Basic Health Care Provision Fund and launched two new NiCare theme songs.

Universal Health Coverage, to me, has always been a political decision before it is a technical one.

Jul 2026

Minna

Advocacy

Advocacy visit to the Office of the First Lady of Niger State

Briefed the First Lady on NiCare's work and the planned BHCPF enrolment drive, alongside the NHIA State Coordinator and a representative of the Niger State Internal Revenue Service. She commended NiCare as one of the leading agencies in the state and agreed to serve as State Health Insurance Ambassador.

Jul 2026

Minna

Award & Partnership

Award of Excellence from the National Health Fellows

The Niger State National Health Fellows (Cohort 2.0) proposed a partnership on community sensitisation, enrolment support, field-level data collection and monitoring across all 25 LGAs, and presented him with an Award of Excellence for strengthening healthcare delivery and expanding health insurance coverage.

The National Health Fellows have a unique advantage of being close to the communities. Their involvement will complement our efforts by providing independent feedback, strengthening accountability, and supporting evidence-based interventions.

Jun 2026

London, United Kingdom

Technology

London Tech Week 2026

Joined innovators, technology leaders and policymakers from around the world at London Tech Week, exchanging ideas on technology and the future of public services.

Jun 2026

Niger State

Quality Assurance

140 health facilities assessed in the Q1 2026 quality assurance exercise

NiCare's first-quarter Quality Assurance, Monitoring and Evaluation exercise covered 128 BHCPF and 12 TISHIP facilities across all 25 LGAs, assessing patient care, records, essential services and adherence to programme guidelines. It was the first exercise to include TISHIP facilities.

May 2026

Bida

Policy Dialogue

Advancing UHC through Joint Strategic LGA Leadership

Co-organised, with the Niger State Ministry of Health and World Bank support, a two-day policy dialogue (7–8 May) for LGA Chairmen, Directors of PHC and health sector stakeholders, and joined the panel on Navigating Financial Autonomy at LGA Level. The dialogue ended with actionable recommendations and a peer-learning network among zonal coordinators.

Apr 2026

Minna

Digital Health

Clinify E-Claims fully integrated into NiCare operations

NiCare processed capitation payments on the Clinify E-Claims platform and trained staff on digital claims, referrals and pre-authorisation. Clinify credited his leadership for championing the Agency's digital transformation.

Apr 2026

Minna

Data Systems

Annual Data Quality Management Meeting for BHCPF 2.0

NiCare convened stakeholders to implement the revised Basic Health Care Provision Fund (BHCPF 2.0), including national reporting through the NHIA Gateway, the National Identification Number as a unique enrollee identifier and a move to real-time electronic reporting.

Mar 2026

Lokoja

Inter-State Collaboration

Courtesy visit to the Kogi State Health Insurance Agency

Visited his counterpart, Dr Aledare Adekunle, to exchange best practices on health insurance coverage and service delivery, emphasising collaboration among state agencies in advancing universal health coverage.

Feb 2026

Accra, Ghana

Study Tour

Study tour of the National Health Insurance Authority of Ghana

Joined fellow CEOs of State Social Health Insurance Agencies at Ghana's NHIA, hosted by Chief Executive Dr Victor Bampoe, drawing transferable lessons on coverage expansion, claims management and system efficiency.

Jan 2026

Accra, Ghana

Leadership Retreat

CEOs of State Social Health Insurance Agencies leadership retreat

Took part in a retreat reflecting on collective progress toward universal health coverage, including leadership training facilitated by Dr Lance Mambondiani and Dr Femi Funsho-Adebayo.
